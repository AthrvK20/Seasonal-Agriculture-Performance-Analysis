# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes agricultural performance across different seasons using data analysis and visualization techniques.

The analysis focuses on identifying seasonal patterns, variations, and relationships in agricultural performance by examining crop yield, environmental conditions, farming practices, resource usage, and economic outcomes.

## Problem Statement

Agricultural performance can vary across seasons due to differences in environmental conditions, farming practices, resource availability, and economic factors.

This project investigates these seasonal differences and identifies meaningful patterns, relationships, and variations within the available agricultural dataset.

## Objectives

- Explore and understand the agricultural dataset.
- Clean and prepare the data for analysis.
- Compare agricultural performance across seasons.
- Analyze crop performance and resource usage.
- Examine relationships between agricultural and economic variables.
- Identify meaningful patterns and variations.
- Develop data-driven insights and recommendations.

## Dataset

The dataset contains **4,000 records and 28 variables** covering different aspects of agricultural activities.

The data includes information related to:

- Crops and seasons
- Environmental conditions
- Irrigation and water usage
- Farming inputs
- Yield and production
- Revenue, cost, and profit
- Disease and pest risk

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed

The project includes:

- Data exploration and cleaning
- Descriptive statistical analysis
- Univariate and bivariate analysis
- Seasonal comparisons
- Crop performance analysis
- Resource and irrigation analysis
- Economic analysis
- Correlation analysis
- Additional analysis of irrigation profitability, crop profitability, and disease/pest risk

## Key Findings

- Kharif showed better overall performance compared with Rabi and Zaid in terms of average yield and profitability.
- Zaid recorded lower average profitability and water efficiency compared with the other seasons.
- Crop profitability varied considerably, with Sugarcane and Chilli having higher profitability rates in the available data.
- Yield and production showed a strong positive relationship, while revenue and profit were also strongly related.
- The dataset contained considerable variation and potential outliers in several agricultural and economic variables.

## Recommendations

- Consider seasonal performance when planning agricultural activities.
- Evaluate irrigation methods together with seasonal conditions.
- Consider both yield and profitability when selecting crops.
- Improve water-use planning and efficiency.
- Consider multiple agricultural and economic factors when making decisions.
